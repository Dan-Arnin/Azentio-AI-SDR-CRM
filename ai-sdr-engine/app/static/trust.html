AI safeguards

AI that drafts. People who decide.

The assistant researches and drafts. Your team stays in charge of what is said, to whom, and when.

  • A person approves every message before it leaves

    Nothing is sent automatically. Each email is reviewed, edited if needed and approved by a named member of your sales team.

    DraftedReviewed by a personApprovedSent
    Enforced on every message

  • Only approved product claims

    Messages draw on a library of statements signed off by Azentio product and legal teams. Nothing else is claimed.

    Enforced

  • No pricing, discounts or commitments

    Commercial terms are never written by AI. Drafts that mention them are blocked and passed to a person.

    Enforced

  • Qualification backed by the buyer's own words

    Budget, decision maker, need and timeline are only marked as confirmed with a direct quote from the buyer.

    Enforced

  • Opt-outs honoured instantly

    One opt-out stops all outreach to that person across email, LinkedIn and text, straight away.

    Enforced

  • Research from public sources only

    Prospect research uses public information, and every fact is linked to the page it came from.

    Enforced

  • Your data never trains models

    Customer and prospect data is never used to train AI models, ours or anyone else's. Contractually guaranteed.

    Contractual
